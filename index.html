Frequently asked

The obvious questions.

The ones you'd ask before downloading. The ones we ask ourselves.

How do you make money? +

We price postcard packs close to cost because the point is to make sending feel casual. Over time, Mailroom can make money from larger packs, gift add-ons, and other ways people keep in touch.

Do I need my friend's address? +

No. Send your friend a link from the app and they can share their address with you privately. They fill it in once, you never see it directly, and we use it to mail the card. Their address stays theirs. (You can also enter an address yourself if you already have one.)

What happens when a friend gets a postcard from me? +

Every Mailroom postcard has a small QR code on the back. When your friend scans it, they're invited to download the app, and we'll give them a free card to send back to you. No subscription, no commitment, just a way for the magic to keep going in both directions. The going-back-and-forth is the whole point.

Is this actually a real postcard or a digital thing? +

Real. Physical. Printed on cardstock, mailed with USPS postage, delivered by your mail carrier. It shows up in your friend's actual mailbox, addressed by name. You can stick it on a fridge. You can tear it. You can frame it. It exists.

Do I really get a free card? +

Yes. One free photo postcard when you join. Printed, mailed, on us. Send it to your best friend, your mom, whoever. No credit card required to claim it. After that, packs start at $5.

How long does it take to arrive? +

A week, end-to-end. We print and drop in the mail within 1-2 business days of you sending. USPS First-Class delivery takes another 3-5 business days from there. Faster if you're in a major metro, slower for rural addresses.

Can I send to multiple people at once? +

Yes. Pick a photo, write a note, and send to as many friends as you want. One credit per recipient. Great for party invites, birth announcements, "I miss you" sends to your whole crew, or just sharing a moment with everyone who was there.

What happens if it doesn't get delivered? +

If a card comes back as undeliverable, we let you know and credit you for another send. If you have the address right and it just gets lost in the mail (rare but happens), email us and we'll resend.

Who's behind this? +

A small team that misses getting mail from people they love. We think a photo on someone's wall is the strongest signal of friendship there is, and we want to make it preposterously easy to send one. Get in touch. We read everything.
