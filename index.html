How it works

Pick. Write. Send.

Text us a photo. Write a note. We pair you with a stranger somewhere across the country — or send to a friend, your call. Either way, a real paper card lands in someone's mailbox in about a week. And one comes back to you.

Pick your photo

The one from that trip you almost didn't take. The blurry one she'll laugh at. The one that's been sitting in your camera roll waiting to mean something.

Write something real

A line, a paragraph, a memory, a joke. Whatever you'd say if you ran into them tomorrow.

Send a private link

Don't have your friend's address? Send them a Mailroom link by text or DM. They fill it in once, privately. You never see it. We use it to mail their card.

We mail it

Printed on real cardstock and mailed with USPS postage within two business days. It lands in their mailbox about a week later.
